# Sample Input — vendor-evaluation

**Evaluation:** CRM Platform Selection
**Organization:** GreenBuild S.A. — B2B construction materials supplier, 200 employees, €45M revenue
**Decision needed by:** October 31, 2026
**Decision maker:** CEO + CFO (joint decision)

**Vendors shortlisted (3):**
1. Salesforce Sales Cloud (Enterprise tier)
2. HubSpot CRM (Enterprise)
3. Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales

**Evaluation criteria (from internal stakeholder survey):**

Must-have:
- Integration with existing SAP ERP (main business system)
- Mobile app for field sales (12 reps visit clients on-site)
- Polish language support
- GDPR-compliant data storage in EU

Important (weighted):
- Ease of use for non-technical sales team (weight: 30%)
- Price (total cost of ownership, 3 years) (weight: 25%)
- SAP integration quality (weight: 20%)
- Reporting and forecasting capability (weight: 15%)
- Customer support quality (weight: 10%)

**Background:**
GreenBuild currently uses a mix of Excel and Outlook for CRM functions. The team is non-technical — most reps are 40–55 years old with limited appetite for complex tools. The Head of Sales (Krzysztof) tried Salesforce at a previous company and said "it was too complicated for a normal person." Budget is tight — CFO wants to keep total 3-year TCO under €80,000.

**Additional context:**
- SAP integration: currently using SAP Business One (not S/4HANA) — niche integration requirement
- Company has no dedicated IT staff — IT support is outsourced (4h/week SLA)
- No plans for marketing automation; just sales pipeline management needed
