## 🆕 What's New in Q3

### Discount Approvals — Now Automated

If you need to offer a discount above 15%, you no longer need to call your manager. Submit the discount request directly in Salesforce and you'll get an answer within 2 hours — automatically routed to the right approver, tracked, logged.

**How it works:**
- Open your Opportunity → go to the Approval Requests section
- Submit a new discount request with the % and a brief justification
- You'll receive an email notification when it's approved or declined
- Your manager sees the full history in SF — no more "did you send that email?"

---

### Product Certification Status — Now Visible on Every Quote

Polish hospitals and clinics increasingly require CE marking verification before raising a PO. We've added a new **Certification Status** field to every product on your Opportunity Line Items — you can now see instantly whether a product has Polish CE marking, without calling the product team.

Look for it in the Products table on any Opportunity. If you see "Verification Required" — contact product management before sending the offer.

### 12 New Email Templates in Your Inbox

We've added 12 ready-to-use email templates to both Salesforce Activities and your Outlook, organized by contact type:

- **Hospital procurement contacts** — formal, reference-friendly

**To use them in SF:** Open an Activity on any Contact → New Email → select a template from the library.

## 💡 Tips This Quarter

### Fill in "Next Step" — it unblocks your manager

Since we made "Next Step" required on Opportunities in Qualification and above, about 15% of opportunities still have it blank (it was possible to close the dialog without filling it in — we've now fixed that).

**Why it matters:** Pipeline reviews rely on Next Step to know who's doing what and when. If it's blank, your deal looks stuck — even if it isn't.

**Quick rule:** Next Step = the one specific action you will take before this opportunity moves forward. Not "follow up" — but "send revised proposal by Friday" or "demo scheduled for Oct 3."

---

### Create a Contact in One Step

You can now add a new Contact and link it to an Account without leaving the Opportunity page.

On any Opportunity → Related Contacts section → **New Contact** button → fill in the form → Save. The Contact is automatically associated with the Opportunity's Account.

Previously you had to go to the Account, create the Contact there, then come back to the Opportunity. This saves 3–4 clicks per new contact.
