## Page 2: IT Setup Guide

### Getting started on Day 1

This guide takes about 45–60 minutes. Follow the steps in order — each one depends on the previous.

---

> **⚡ Quick Start — The 5 things you must do first:**
> 1. Power on your laptop and connect to the guest WiFi (`Novatek-Guest`, no password)
> 2. Log into Microsoft 365 with your email and temporary password (sent to your personal email)
> 3. Change your password immediately after first login
> 4. Install the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone (required for login)
> 5. Set up MFA (multi-factor authentication) — you'll be prompted automatically

---

### Step 1 — Log into your Microsoft 365 account

1. Open a browser and go to: **portal.office.com**
2. Enter your work email: `firstname.lastname@novatek.pl`
3. Enter the temporary password from the welcome email sent to your personal address
4. You'll be asked to change your password — choose something at least 12 characters long
5. Set up Microsoft Authenticator when prompted — you'll need this for all future logins

> **Tip:** If you didn't receive the welcome email, check spam. If it's not there, email hr@novatek.pl — they'll resend it.

---

### Step 2 — Connect to company WiFi

Once your M365 account is active:

1. Disconnect from `Novatek-Guest`
2. Connect to `Novatek-Corp` using your new work email and password
3. This network gives you access to internal systems, printers, and shared drives

---

### Step 3 — Install required software

Open the **Company Portal** app (pre-installed on your laptop) and install:

| App | Why you need it |
|---|---|
| Microsoft Teams | All internal communication and video calls |
| Outlook | Company email |
| GlobalProtect VPN | Required for working remotely |
| Slack | Project-specific team channels (ask your manager which workspaces to join) |

*Each installation takes 2–5 minutes. Do not restart until all are installed.*

---

### Step 4 — Set up VPN (for remote work)

You'll need this from your first remote day.

1. Open **GlobalProtect** (installed in Step 3)
2. Enter the portal address: `vpn.novatek.pl`
3. Log in with your M365 credentials
4. Click "Connect"

Test it: try opening an internal SharePoint page from outside the office.

---

### Step 5 — Verify your email and calendar

1. Open Outlook on your laptop
2. Your calendar should already have your first week's meetings
3. If you don't see any meetings, email hr@novatek.pl — your account may need a manual sync

## Page 3: HR Policies — What You Need to Know

### The short version of the key policies

We know nobody wants to read a 40-page policy document on their first week. Here's what matters most.

---

### 🏖️ Vacation

| Year | Days |
|---|---|
| First year (probationary period, first 6 months) | 20 days |
| From 7th month onward | 26 days |

- Request vacation in **Workday** (HR system) — your manager approves it there
- Carry-over: up to 10 unused days can be carried to the following year (use them by March 31)
- Public holidays (Polish, 13 per year) are on top of your vacation days

---

### 🏠 Remote Work

- **During probation (first 3 months):** Please come to the office at least 3 days/week. This is a recommendation, not a hard rule — talk to your manager.
- **After probation:** Up to 3 remote days per week. The other days are office days.
- **Fully remote:** By agreement with your manager; not the default for most roles.

To work remotely: connect via VPN, be available on Teams during working hours, respond to messages within 30 minutes.

---

### 💳 Expense Reimbursement

If you spend money on behalf of Novatek (travel, equipment, client entertainment):

1. Keep your receipt (photo is fine)
2. Submit via **Workday → Expense Reports** within 30 days
3. Approval from your manager
4. Reimbursement in the next payroll cycle

**Pre-approvals required for:** flights, accommodation, equipment purchases over PLN 300.

Questions? Contact: expenses@novatek.pl

### 👔 Dress Code

Business casual. We're a professional company but we don't wear suits. Jeans are fine. Client meetings on-site: smart-casual (no hoodies).

## Page 4: FAQ — Questions Everyone Asks

**Where do I find the office WiFi password?**
You don't need one for `Novatek-Guest`. For `Novatek-Corp`, use your M365 login credentials. See IT Setup Guide → Step 2.

---

**I didn't receive my temporary password. What do I do?**
Check your personal email's spam folder. If it's not there, contact hr@novatek.pl or call ext. 102.

---

**How do I book a meeting room?**
Open Outlook → Calendar → New Meeting → add a room (search "Warsaw" to see available rooms). Rooms can be booked up to 30 days in advance.

---

**How do I print?**
Printers are connected automatically when you're on `Novatek-Corp` WiFi. Open any document, click Print, and select the nearest printer (they're labeled by floor).

---

**Where are shared files / company documents?**
SharePoint → [Documents library link]. Most teams also use Teams for file sharing — ask your manager where your team stores files.

---

**Can I use personal devices for work?**
For communication: yes (Teams, email on your phone). For company files and systems: only from company-issued devices or via VPN + browser. Do not install company software on personal laptops.

---

**Who do I contact for IT issues?**
Email: it-help@novatek.pl | Phone: ext. 100 | Hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00

---

**How do I request access to a system I don't have?**
Email it-help@novatek.pl with: system name, your manager's name and approval, and why you need access. Standard turnaround: 1 business day.
