# Sample Input — ai-readiness-assessment

**Company:** ProMetal Sp. z o.o.
**Industry:** Metal fabrication / manufacturing
**Size:** ~320 employees (2 factories, Warsaw HQ)

**AI initiative being considered:**
Board mandated an "AI strategy" after reading competitor press releases. Specific ideas floated: predictive maintenance on CNC machines, AI-assisted quality control (vision cameras), demand forecasting for raw materials. No formal project exists yet.

**What triggered this assessment:**
New CEO hired from a digital-native company wants to understand baseline before committing budget. CFO skeptical — previous ERP upgrade ran 40% over budget and took 3 years instead of 18 months.

**Sponsor:** COO — Marek Żurawski

**People / Skills:**
No data scientists, no ML engineers. IT team: 4 people (sysadmin, 2 developers, helpdesk). 2 developers use SQL and build internal reports. Business intelligence: mostly Excel dashboards built by a Finance analyst.

**Data:**
- MES (Manufacturing Execution System) — collects machine sensor data but "nobody really uses it"
- ERP (Comarch Optima) — financials, orders, materials; data goes back 8 years
- Quality control: paper checklists, not digitized
- Machine maintenance logs: partly in ERP, partly in Excel files per factory manager

**Culture:**
Failed ERP upgrade created significant change fatigue. Middle managers resistant. Shop floor workers skeptical of digital initiatives. IT department seen as bottleneck by business.

**Timeline pressure:**
Board wants "AI roadmap" presented at Q4 board meeting (12 weeks away).
