# Sample Input — workshop-facilitator

**Workshop:** Loyalty Programme Redesign — Requirements Workshop
**Duration:** 3 hours
**Date:** 2026-10-08, 09:00–12:00
**Location:** HQ Warsaw, Room Atlas + Zoom for remote participants

**Goal:** Define MVP scope and key decisions for the new loyalty programme that replaces the current points system (12 years old, no mobile, 4% active user rate)

**Participants (12 people):**
- 3 Marketing (including Marketing Director — main sponsor)
- 2 IT (lead developer + IT architect)
- 1 Finance (cost modelling)
- 2 Customer Experience (CX analyst, CX lead)
- 2 Sales (B2C channel managers)
- 2 Customer representatives (invited from customer panel)

**Pre-workshop context:**
- Previous loyalty programme had 4% monthly active users (industry avg: 22%)
- Customer survey (n=800): top complaints = "too hard to understand how points work", "rewards not relevant to me", "can't use points on mobile"
- Budget: €320,000 for new programme build (tight)
- Constraint: IT says minimum 6 months to build; Marketing wants launch before Christmas 2026 (not achievable per IT — tension exists)

**Key decisions to make in the workshop:**
1. Points vs tiers vs hybrid model
2. What "MVP" means (what's in, what's out for launch)
3. Mobile: native app or web only for MVP?
4. Partner rewards (discount vouchers from external brands) — in scope for launch or phase 2?

**Facilitator concerns:**
- Marketing Director tends to dominate; customer voice needs protection
- IT and Marketing have different expectations on timeline — this needs to surface and be resolved
- Workshop must end with documented decisions, not just discussion
