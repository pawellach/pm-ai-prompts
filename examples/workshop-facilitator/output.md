## Facilitation Scripts

### 09:00 — Welcome + Ground Rules (15 min)

> "Welcome everyone, including those joining remotely — we can see and hear you. Today we have 3 hours to make 4 key decisions about the loyalty programme MVP. We're not here to debate the full 3-year vision — we're here to agree on what we build first and by when.
>
> Ground rules for today:
> 1. One voice at a time — use the raise-hand feature online, give space in the room.
> 2. We represent different functions, but we have one goal: a programme that customers actually use.
> 3. Today's decisions will be documented. If we leave a decision unresolved, that's fine — but we'll name who owns it and by when.
> 4. **Customer voice is primary.** We have two customers with us today. Their input shapes everything else."

---

### 09:30 — Customer Voice Exercise (30 min)

**Setup:** Divide into 3 groups (mix functions). Customer panel members split between groups.

**Prompt (on cards):** 
*"Think of a time you used a loyalty programme you loved — or hated. What made it work or fail? What are your top 2 things a loyalty programme must do to be worth your time?"*

Groups discuss (10 min), then each group shares top 2 insights (10 min total), facilitator clusters themes on the board (10 min).

**Facilitation note:** If Marketing Director starts steering toward features before customers speak — pause: *"Let's hear from [customer name] first — you've used our current programme, right? What would you personally change?"*

**Expected clusters:** Simplicity of earning, Value of rewards, Mobile access, Speed to redemption.

---

### 10:00 — Decision 1: Programme Model (30 min)

**Format: Dot voting + 15-min discussion**

Present 3 options on flipchart/Miro:

| Option | Description | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: Points | Earn points per purchase, redeem for rewards | Familiar, low switching cost for customers | Current model has 4% active rate — familiarity didn't help |
| B: Tiers | Silver / Gold / Platinum based on spend | Drives aspiration; simple to understand | Requires longer engagement to see rewards; less immediate |
| C: Hybrid | Tiers + points within each tier | Best of both; flexible | More complex to explain and build |

**Dot vote:** Each participant gets 3 dots (Miro: reaction stickers). Vote for your preferred option.

**Discussion (15 min):** Focus on "what does our customer survey tell us?" — simplicity was the #1 complaint. A complex hybrid may repeat the same mistake.

**Decision target:** Facilitator calls for a show of hands after discussion. If split, ask Marketing Director (sponsor) to make the call.

---

### 10:45 — Decision 2: MVP Scope (30 min)

**Format: In / Out / Maybe wall**

On a board (physical or Miro), three columns: IN | OUT | MAYBE

Read out feature list one by one (pre-prepared); group calls which column. Features:
- Earn points/status on purchases ✓ ask
- Redeem rewards in-store ✓ ask
- Redeem rewards online ✓ ask
- Mobile app (iOS/Android) ✓ ask
- Web portal (desktop) ✓ ask
- Push notifications ✓ ask
- Partner brand vouchers ✓ ask
- Personalized rewards ✓ ask
- Social sharing ("I just earned Gold!") ✓ ask
- Customer dashboard (balance, history) ✓ ask
- Admin panel for Marketing ✓ ask

**Facilitation rule:** Anything that starts a debate goes to MAYBE. We decide MAYBE items at the end of the exercise.

**For MAYBE items:** Quick round-robin — "In or out? 30 seconds each, no speeches." Then majority rules.

---

### 11:15 — Decisions 3 & 4 (20 min)

**Decision 3: Mobile — native app vs responsive web for MVP**

Quick poll + IT input:
- Native app: +3 months to timeline; €60k additional cost
- Responsive web: works on all devices; same codebase; faster to build

Ask IT lead to give a 2-minute technical summary. Then vote.

**Decision 4: Partner rewards — launch or phase 2?**

Ask: "Can we launch a successful loyalty programme without partner vouchers?"
- If yes → Phase 2
- If no → In scope, but budget needs to be reallocated

---

### 11:35 — Timeline Resolution (20 min)

This is the highest-risk conversation. Pre-frame:

> "Before we close, there's an elephant in the room. Marketing has communicated a Christmas 2026 launch. IT says minimum 6 months from now. We're in October. I want to spend 20 minutes resolving this honestly, because launching with the wrong expectations would be worse than launching later."

**Structured process:**

1. IT lead: "What is the fastest realistic timeline, and what has to be true for that?" (5 min)
2. Marketing Director: "What is the minimum viable Christmas milestone — does it have to be the full programme, or would a waitlist/teaser count?" (5 min)
3. Discussion: Is there a partial launch option (e.g., pilot with 1,000 customers in December; full launch Q1 2027)? (8 min)
4. Document the agreement (2 min)

**Facilitator posture:** Don't mediate — surface the decision. The Marketing Director has the authority to say "we launch in Q1 instead of Christmas." That's fine. The workshop just needs an honest agreement.
