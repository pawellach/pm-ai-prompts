# Sample Input — technical-discovery-facilitator

**Client:** Norda Retail S.A.
**Industry:** Retail — 18 stores across Poland + e-commerce
**Engagement:** 3-month systems integration and data consolidation project

**Why the client hired us:**
Store managers make purchasing decisions based on gut feel and phone calls with warehouse staff. There is no unified view of inventory across stores + warehouse + e-commerce. The board wants to launch a "data-driven replenishment" initiative but the CTO doesn't know where to start.

**Known systems (from pre-sales call):**
- **Symphony ERP** — custom-built 12 years ago; handles financials and central warehouse inventory; no documentation
- **18× Retail POS** — mix: 10 stores on Comarch CDN, 8 on a legacy DOS-era system from 2002 (locally called "the dinosaur")
- **Shopify Plus** — e-commerce, launched 2 years ago
- **Warehouse WMS** — Excel-based, managed by the warehouse manager; "our biggest data quality problem"

**Key contacts:**
- Piotr Nowak, CTO — our main contact; technically competent, wants to fix the mess
- Beata Wróbel, Warehouse Director — owns the Excel WMS; described by CTO as "protective of her processes"
- Jan Kowalczyk, Head of E-commerce — champion; frustrated by lack of inventory data in Shopify

**Known issues (from pre-sales):**
- Store managers manually email their stock requests to the warehouse — no system integration
- E-commerce oversells products that are out of stock in the warehouse because Shopify is not connected to ERP
- Comarch CDN stores send daily CSV files to HQ; 8 DOS-era stores send paper reports weekly

**Engagement goal per sponsor (CEO):** By end of engagement, want a "single source of truth" for inventory.
