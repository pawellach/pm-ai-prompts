# Sample Input — stakeholder-comms-planner

**Project:** SAP S/4HANA Migration — Finance & Procurement modules
**Organization:** Polaris Manufacturing S.A., 800 employees
**Timeline:** 8 months (Oct 2026 — May 2027)
**Sponsor:** CEO — Jacek Wiśniewski

**Stakeholders to manage:**
- **Jacek Wiśniewski (CEO)** — project sponsor; wants progress updates, not technical details; very busy; prefers 1-pagers
- **Piotr Krawczyk (CFO)** — owns the Finance module migration; initially skeptical (last ERP project went 60% over budget); needs to trust the numbers
- **Ewa Nowak (Head of Finance, 120 FTEs)** — her team will be most disrupted; 40% of transactions will change; very vocal, influential in the organization
- **IT department (led by Michał Dąbrowski)** — technical owner; supportive but understable; limited bandwidth (running 3 other projects)
- **Procurement team (25 people, leader: Tomasz Bielak)** — light users; main concern is that purchase order process doesn't break
- **SAP implementation partner (Accenture team)** — external; needs timely decisions from Polaris to stay on schedule
- **Works Council** — must be informed per Polish labor law; any change to roles/processes requires consultation

**Key risks for communication:**
- Ewa Nowak's team is change-resistant; she has the ear of the CFO
- Works Council involvement was forgotten in planning — they found out through the grapevine; currently unhappy
- IT is under-resourced; Michał has not formally committed bandwidth

**Communication objective:** Keep all stakeholders informed, manage resistance, ensure decisions arrive on time to not delay the Accenture team.
