## 3. Key Messages by Stakeholder

### CEO (Jacek Wiśniewski)
> "This migration positions Polaris to scale finance and procurement operations without adding headcount. We are on track. Here's where we need your decision."

Keep it to: status (green/amber/red), one key risk, one decision needed.

### CFO (Piotr Krawczyk)
> "The budget is under control. Here is where we stand vs. forecast. Here is the Finance module risk and how we're managing it."

Piotr was burned before — lead every communication with budget actuals and variance explanation. Show you know the numbers. Don't minimize issues; surface them early with mitigation.

### Head of Finance (Ewa Nowak)
> "Your team will be ready for go-live. We know this changes how you work — here's exactly what changes, here's what stays the same, and here's when your team will be trained."

Ewa's resistance is fear of the unknown and fear of her team being exposed. Give her specifics early and often. Involve her team leads as UAT champions — turning change agents into "insiders" reduces resistance.

### Works Council
> "We should have brought you in earlier — that was an oversight. Here is the formal consultation on the process changes. Here's how we plan to support affected employees."

The Works Council situation needs a repair conversation before the formal consultation. Acknowledge the oversight directly. They are more likely to be cooperative if they feel heard.

## 6. Escalation Path

1. Issues within project team → PM resolves or escalates to workstream lead
2. Issues with Accenture (delays, scope disputes) → PM escalates to project steering committee within 5 business days
3. Issues with Works Council formal consultation → PM + HR + Legal
4. Budget overrun > 10% → CFO notified immediately; CEO notified if > 20%
5. Go-live risk → Steering committee emergency session
