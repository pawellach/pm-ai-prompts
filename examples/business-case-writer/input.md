# Sample Input — business-case-writer

**Initiative:** AI-powered customer support deflection — implement an intelligent self-service portal with AI chat to reduce inbound support volume

**Company:** NovaSaaS — B2B SaaS, ~180k customers, 45 support agents, ARR €28M

**The problem:**
Support team receives ~9,000 tickets/month. 62% are tier-1 questions: password resets, billing queries, basic how-to questions. Each ticket costs ~€22 to resolve. Support team is growing headcount to keep up — 2 new hires planned for Q1.

**Business goal:**
Deflect 40% of inbound tickets (target: 3,600/month) within 6 months of go-live. Avoid Q1 headcount addition.

**Sponsor:** VP Product — Joanna Adamska
**Decision-maker:** CFO + CEO — decision needed by Oct 15

**Investment range:** ~€180,000 one-time + ~€40,000/year ongoing

**Alternatives considered:**
1. Do nothing — continue hiring agents
2. Buy Intercom Fin (AI chat) — faster but limited customization, €60k/year licensing
3. Build in-house AI chat on company knowledge base — recommended path

**Timeline:** Q4 decision → Q1 build → Q2 launch

**Data available:**
- Support ticket categorization data (18 months)
- Resolution time by category
- CSAT by channel (email 3.8, chat 4.1)
- Headcount cost model available from HR
- Intercom Fin case studies: avg 35–45% deflection rate
