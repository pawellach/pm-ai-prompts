# Sample Input — project-kickoff-facilitator

**Project:** AI-Powered Demand Forecasting
**Organization:** NordFood S.A. — food distribution company, 1,200 employees, 3 warehouses
**Timeline:** 6 months (October 2026 — March 2027)
**Budget:** €400,000 (approved)
**Kickoff date:** October 6, 2026

**Team (8 people):**
- Agnieszka Wieczorek — Project Manager (internal)
- 3 Data Scientists (2 internal + 1 contractor)
- 2 Data Engineers (internal)
- 1 Business Analyst / change manager (internal)
- 1 DevOps Engineer (contractor)

**Sponsors and stakeholders:**
- CEO — executive sponsor; wants AI forecast to replace "gut feel" procurement
- Head of Supply Chain — business owner; will use the tool daily; skeptical (30 years experience, trusts his instincts)
- IT Director — technical oversight; worried about data quality
- Finance — cost of overstock/stockouts is their problem; supportive

**Project background:**
- Current state: demand forecasting done manually by supply chain team; Excel-based, uses historical data + experience
- Annual overstock cost: ~€2.8M/year (perishables discarded)
- Annual stockout cost: ~€1.1M/year (lost sales)
- AI model target: reduce forecast error from 22% (current) to <10%
- Integration target: forecast outputs fed into ERP (SAP) procurement module automatically

**Key risks identified pre-kickoff:**
- Data quality: historical order data has gaps from ERP migration 3 years ago
- Head of Supply Chain resistance: his team may not trust AI predictions
- SAP integration complexity underestimated in scoping

**What the kickoff should achieve:**
- Team aligned on goals, scope, and ways of working
- Stakeholders understand what they'll be asked to contribute
- Head of Supply Chain engaged (not just informed)
- Risks tabled and owned
- First sprint planned
